How it works

Three steps. You're in control of every one.

01

Swipe to review

One photo at a time. Swipe right to keep a memory, left to set one aside for review. Undo anything, anytime.

Keep →
02

Review the list

Nothing is gone yet. Everything you set aside lands in a review list where you can look again and pull photos back out.

← Review to delete
03

Confirm to delete

Only when you tap confirm does PhotoLess remove the selected photos. Recoverable from Recently Deleted, as always.

Good to know

Questions, answered plainly.

Does swiping delete my photos right away?

No. Swiping only adds a photo to a review list. Nothing is removed until you look at the list and tap confirm.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

Never. PhotoLess has no servers and no cloud. Your photos are read on your device, only to show them to you.

Does it use AI to look at my photos?

No AI ever sees your photos. PhotoLess just shows them to you in a calm, one-at-a-time flow so you decide.

Can I get a photo back after deleting?

Deletions go through your device's standard flow, so removed photos usually sit in “Recently Deleted” for a while before they're gone for good.

Is PhotoLess free?

Yes — daily review sessions are free, and safety features (undo, review, confirm) always are. Premium unlocks unlimited review and the Studio tools.

Do I need an account?

No account, no email, no login. Open the app and start — PhotoLess never knows who you are.
